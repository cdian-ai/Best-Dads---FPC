Set up their rewards

There's a starter list ready to go. Two things worth knowing about how it's priced.

🍫
Time together is cheap on purposeTwenty minutes one-to-one costs 25 points; a packet of crisps costs 250. That’s the whole idea — the good stuff should be the easy stuff.
🎲
Some rewards are limited, not dearA trip to the park is once a week. That way it stays affordable without costing you every Saturday.
💛
Points aren't the pointThe app will nudge you now and then with ideas for connecting with your children — that's what makes the rest of it work.

Just the first week

You don’t have to use all of it. Do these three and the rest makes sense on its own.

1️⃣
Award something todayAnything, however small. The first point is the one that makes it real for them.
2️⃣
Say what it was for, out loud“That was kind of you — three points.” The words do more work than the number.
3️⃣
Let a request come in, and answer itYes or no both work. They learn how the loop runs either way.
